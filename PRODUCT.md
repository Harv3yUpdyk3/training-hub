# PRODUCT.md: Will's Training Hub

Read this before DESIGN.md. It says who the hub is for and what it must never do.

## Who it's for

One rider: Will, in Atlanta, riding a Specialized Diverge (training) and a Bombtrack Arise (relaxed touring). He checks the hub on his Android phone as an installed app, most days, often right before or after a ride. A desktop browser is the occasional second screen.

## What it's for

1. **This week:** what to ride today, the plan for the week, and whether the weather changes it.
2. **The season:** 5,000 km in 2026, Eddington 55 in miles, and the build toward the Col du Grand Colombier and the Col du Tourmalet in July 2027.
3. **Proof:** what he actually rode, from Strava, compared with the plan.
4. **Trips:** week-long tours matched to each bike.

## Principles

- The real numbers first. Strava data, the plan and the forecast; say when a figure is an estimate.
- One glance answers "what do I ride today?"
- Fewer, better charts. No speed-trend, time-of-day or weekday charts: they cause analysis paralysis.
- Phone first. Nothing scrolls sideways at 360px.
- Coach the Diverge by heart rate (and power once the meter is on). Never coach the Bombtrack.

## Never

- Never put the Strava Client ID or Secret, or any GitHub token, in the repo. They live only on Will's device.
- Never show the hub's own upgrade list on the site. It lives in the private enhancement plan.
- Never change Will's pinned look (palette, dark green, rings, stars, pictograms) without asking. See DESIGN.md.
