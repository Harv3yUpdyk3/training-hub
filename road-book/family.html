Will at the Tour de France

8 to 26 July 2027. Where he is each day, and when to look for him on TV. All times are US Eastern.

How to watch in the US

Peacock streams every stage live from start to finish. NBC and CNBC show some stages too, mostly the big mountain days and the weekends. Check the listings in June 2027, when the TV schedule is confirmed.

  • France is 6 hours ahead of Eastern, so most stages finish between 10:30am and noon Eastern.
  • The caravan (the sponsor parade) comes through about 1h30 before the riders. It isn't on TV, but it's when Will is grabbing freebies.
  • On the Will sees the riders time, the cameras are on the stretch of road where he's standing. Look for a US flag. On the flat stages the bunch passes in under a minute; in the mountains they're spread out and slower.
